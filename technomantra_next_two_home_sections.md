# TechnoMantra --- Homepage Next Two Sections

## Scope

The cinematic Hero section is **fully complete and must not be
modified**.

This document defines only the next two homepage sections:

1.  **What We Do**
2.  **Services**

The visual direction for both sections must remain:

-   Minimal
-   Clean
-   Premium
-   Professional
-   B2B technology-company quality
-   Strong typography
-   Generous whitespace
-   Restrained motion
-   No visual clutter
-   No unnecessary cards
-   No excessive glassmorphism
-   No cyberpunk/gaming aesthetics
-   No generic SaaS template appearance

The sections should feel like a natural continuation of the existing
premium space/universe Hero, but they should **not depend on the Hero's
3D implementation**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# SECTION 02 --- WHAT WE DO

## Purpose

This section is the immediate explanation after the Hero.

The Hero creates the emotional impression.

This section answers the practical question:

> **What exactly does TechnoMantra do?**

It should establish TechnoMantra as a technology partner that builds
digital products, business systems, and growth-focused digital
solutions.

Do not list every service here.

The detailed service list comes in the next section.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Section Identity

### Eyebrow

`WHAT WE DO`

Small uppercase label with generous letter spacing.

Keep it subtle.

Possible supporting index:

`01`

Use the number only if it fits the existing visual system.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Main Heading

### Primary heading

> **We turn complex ideas into useful digital products.**

This should be the dominant element of the section.

Typography:

-   Large
-   Strong
-   Clean
-   High contrast
-   Maximum width around 800--900px
-   No unnecessary decorative typography

The heading should occupy enough visual space to feel important without
becoming oversized.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Supporting Copy

Use:

> **TechnoMantra builds websites, business software, automation systems
> and digital experiences designed around how modern businesses actually
> work.**

Keep this paragraph relatively narrow, approximately 500--650px.

Use secondary/muted text.

Do not add a large wall of text.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# WHAT WE DO --- VISUAL DIRECTION

The section should use a restrained visual system.

Recommended:

-   Dark background continuing the overall site atmosphere
-   Very subtle radial/ambient lighting if needed
-   Thin separators
-   Fine borders
-   White primary typography
-   Muted gray supporting text
-   Restrained warm-gold accent
-   Very subtle blue atmospheric accent only where appropriate

Avoid:

-   Six large cards
-   Giant icons
-   Stock illustrations
-   Generic gradient blobs
-   Excessive glowing borders
-   Large 3D objects
-   Decorative planets
-   Random floating UI

The section should feel expensive because of **spacing, typography,
proportion and interaction**, not because of visual noise.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# WHAT WE DO --- INTERACTION

Use a very restrained scroll reveal.

Suggested behavior:

### On entering viewport

1.  Eyebrow fades in and moves upward slightly.
2.  Main heading reveals smoothly.
3.  Supporting paragraph follows.
4.  Three capability groups reveal sequentially.

Keep the movement short and subtle.

Suggested timing:

-   400--700ms per element
-   small stagger
-   ease-out movement

Do not create large translations.

Do not pin this section.

Do not create horizontal scrolling.

Do not use 3D effects here.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# WHAT WE DO --- HOVER

Capability groups can have a subtle hover state.

When hovering:

-   title becomes slightly brighter
-   accent line becomes visible
-   description remains readable
-   items become slightly more prominent

Do not:

-   scale the entire section
-   create giant cards
-   create glowing boxes
-   create excessive cursor effects

# SERVICES --- INTERACTION

This is where one of the site's "cool scroll effects" can be used.

Do not make the entire section a flashy animation.

Recommended interaction:

### Scroll-driven service reveal

As each service row enters the viewport:

-   Number becomes brighter
-   Service title moves slightly into position
-   Description reveals
-   Thin separator animates
-   Arrow subtly moves
-   Previous row settles into a quieter state

Only one or a small number of rows should feel visually active at a
time.

This creates the feeling of moving through a list of capabilities
without overwhelming the visitor.

# Responsive Principles

## Desktop

Use the full editorial layouts and subtle interactions.

## Tablet

Reduce spacing and preview areas while preserving hierarchy.

## Mobile

Prioritize:

1.  readability
2.  navigation
3.  service discoverability
4.  performance

Do not simply shrink desktop components.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Technical Principles

When these sections are implemented:

-   Use semantic HTML.
-   Keep content outside Three.js unless genuinely visual.
-   Use CSS/DOM for readable text.
-   Use GSAP only for meaningful scroll choreography.
-   Avoid React state updates every frame.
-   Use refs for animation values.
-   Do not add unnecessary packages.
-   Keep components reusable.
-   Keep service data separate from presentation components.
-   Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
-   Preserve the existing Lenis + ScrollTrigger setup.
-   Do not modify the completed Hero.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Desired Feeling

The first two sections after the Hero should make a visitor think:

> **They understand technology, they understand business, and they can
> actually build the things they are talking about.**

The visual experience should feel:

**quietly impressive, not loudly impressive.**

The objective is a premium technology company website that feels
intentional at every scroll position.
